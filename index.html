About

Residential real estate brokerage serving Massachusetts.

Henry Rowe is a licensed real estate agent in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, operating with The Avenue Team at SERHANT. Apollo Advertising LLC is the operating entity that manages marketing, lead generation, and client communications on Henry's behalf.

SERHANT. is the most followed real estate brand in the world, with a referral network spanning 133 countries and 45,000 plus agents. Henry combines that global platform with on the ground Massachusetts expertise across Greater Boston, MetroWest, and Worcester County.

Services we offer

  • Buyer representation. Property search, financing referrals, showings, offer strategy, and closing coordination for residential buyers.
  • Seller representation. Listing strategy, professional marketing, comparative pricing analysis, open houses, and negotiation through closing for residential sellers.
  • Market analysis and consultations. Comparative market analyses, valuation reports, and one on one strategy meetings for current and prospective property owners.
  • Relocation support. Inbound and outbound relocation through SERHANT's global referral network.

How we work

  1. Initial consultation. A 15 minute introduction call or in person meeting to understand your goals, timeline, and budget. There is no fee or obligation.
  2. Tailored plan. Buyer search criteria or listing strategy, with weekly check ins and full visibility into the work.
  3. Active engagement. Property tours, open houses, market updates, and offer negotiations as the transaction progresses.
  4. Closing. Coordination with attorneys, lenders, inspectors, and the title company through to keys in hand.

Service area

Greater Boston including Watertown, Cambridge, Newton, and Brookline. MetroWest including Sudbury, Lincoln, Concord, Wayland, Wellesley, and Weston. Worcester County including the city of Worcester and surrounding markets. Coastal and Cape Massachusetts on referral.

Hours of operation

Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM ET. Saturday and Sunday by appointment, including open house hours posted in active listings. Eastern Time.
